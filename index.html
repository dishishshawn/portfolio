/research

Epitaxial oxides, and whether the interface is any good.

Since January 2026 I have worked in Dr. Hanjong Paik’s group at OU’s Center for Quantum Research & Technology, where I grow epitaxial oxide and nitride thin films and heterostructures and run the vacuum deposition and measurement hardware that produces them.

My main project is BaTiO3/SrTiO3/Si. I compare high-resolution X-ray diffraction against transport and optical measurements to assess crystalline quality, strain state, and interface behavior — the same sample read by more than one instrument, so a claim about film quality has more than one line of evidence behind it.

Analysis was the bottleneck. The group fit peaks by hand, which made film-quality numbers slow to produce and hard to compare between people. I wrote XRDC, a MATLAB toolkit with rocking-curve and reciprocal-space-map workflows, to make that step reproducible and to standardize how we report it.
